# MUSEUM EXPERIENCE 6: BEFORE THE DOORS

Interactive cultural experience based on the Salon Format concept brief for “BEFORE THE DOORS OPEN”.

Current phase: visual world and opening sequence only.

Scope:

1. Exterior of the Vienna Secession, 15 April 1902
2. Transition into the building
3. First interior moment before the exhibition opens

The concept brief is the source of truth for story, tone, historical integrity, copy and interaction principles. Original Salon Format illustration and handwriting assets will be integrated later; placeholders must remain visibly identified until then.
